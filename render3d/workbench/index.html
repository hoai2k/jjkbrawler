3D pose

character-oriented

Drag a joint and its PARENT bone rotates toward the pointer. Edits land on the pose named above — the drawing beside the model — and every state that draws it gets them; the in-betweens rebuild from the curve. Round handles are the pose's to set; diamond ones ⌖ are aimed at the target by a solver, and their edits are saved as offsets from that solve. Output changes downloads the pose library.

Model size & facing

Turns the whole rig, before every pose layer — for a delivery authored facing some way other than the spec's +Z. Turn it until the model carries itself like the others do (a delivered rig that honours the spec sits at 0°). A free dial, not quarter steps: the wrong-facing rigs that actually arrived were off by 60°, not 90. Both settings ride along in Export payload as renderScale and yawOffsetDeg.
