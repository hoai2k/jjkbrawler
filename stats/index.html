Visitor stats

Who is finding JJK Brawler II, and from where.

What this records

Kept, per visit
  • Country, derived from the IP address
  • Referrer — the page that linked here
  • Browser and operating system
  • Screen width and language
  • Which page, and when
Never kept
  • IP addresses
  • The full User-Agent string
  • Any tracker or advertising ID
  • Cookies — none are set, by us or by the counter
  • Anything about how the game itself was played

Counting is done by GoatCounter, whose privacy page is the authority on the two lists above. Local play is not counted: its script skips localhost, private addresses and file://, so the numbers here are other people, not you testing a stage forty times.
